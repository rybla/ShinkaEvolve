<DESCRIPTION>
To maximize `tm_sizes_average` and `used_vars_proportions_average`, I am implementing two key changes:
1. **Type-Inhabitant Caching**: When generating an `AppTm`, we now prioritize `arg_ty` values that already exist in the context. This ensures that the argument branch of the application has a much higher probability of selecting a variable from the context rather than generating a new literal, pushing the variable reuse metric higher.
2. **Explicit Spine Recursion**: The `gen_term` function is adjusted to favor `AppTm` more aggressively when the budget is high, while maintaining the 75/25 budget split to ensure the left-heavy "spine" structure that drives up the average term size.
3. **Context-Aware Type Generation**: `gen_type` is slightly biased to produce types already seen in the context to increase the density of matching variables.
</DESCRIPTION>
