To improve the `combined_score`, I am implementing a two-pronged strategy:
1. **Depth-Dependent Constructor Weighting**: The constructor weights now shift dynamically as the recursion depth increases. At lower depths (higher budget), we prioritize `App` and `Lam` to build complex structure. As the budget depletes, we pivot to `Var` nodes to ensure variables are used and terms terminate correctly.
2. **Explicit 0.7 Asymmetric Budget Rule**: Instead of `rng.randint(1, budget - 1)`, I am implementing a fixed 70% budget allocation to the function/lambda body branch. This creates deeper, more complex trees which naturally increases term size and unique subterms.
3. **Enhanced Context-Aware Type Selection**: Increased the probability of selecting an `arg_ty` from the existing context to 80% to ensure the argument branch can be satisfied by a `Var` from the context, boosting the `used_vars` metric.
