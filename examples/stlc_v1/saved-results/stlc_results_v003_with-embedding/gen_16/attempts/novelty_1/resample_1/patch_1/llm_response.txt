<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces dynamic weighting based on the remaining budget to push the `tm_sizes_average` higher. By increasing the probability of `App` and `Lam` when the budget is high and tapering off to `Var` and `Lit` as the budget depletes, we can generate deeper, more complex trees.
