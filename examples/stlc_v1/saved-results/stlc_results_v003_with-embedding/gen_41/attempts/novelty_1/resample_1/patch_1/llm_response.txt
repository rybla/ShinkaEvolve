<DESCRIPTION>
This optimization introduces depth-dependent constructor weighting and reinforces cubic recency bias. By scaling the likelihood of `App` and `Lam` nodes higher when the budget is large (at the top of the tree) and transitioning to `Var` selection as the budget diminishes, we create more complex functional structures. It also ensures the "Redex-Variable Synergy" is applied even in standard applications when a context-matching variable is available, further boosting `used_vars`.
</DESCRIPTION>
