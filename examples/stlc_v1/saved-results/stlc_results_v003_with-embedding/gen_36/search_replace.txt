<DESCRIPTION>
Optimizes the generator to reach the 12.7+ score bracket by:
1.  **Upgrading to Cubic Recency Bias**: Replacing quadratic `(i+1)**2` with cubic `(i+1)**3` weights for variable selection. This creates a much stronger pressure to reuse local variables, directly boosting `used_vars_proportions_average`.
2.  **Budget-Scaled Redex Probability**: Scaling the redex chance dynamically based on remaining budget (`0.55 + budget/150`) to ensure higher redex density in large terms.
3.  **Strengthening CATS and Forced Argument selection**: Increasing the probability of selecting argument types from context to 85% and the "forced variable argument" within redexes to 90%.
4.  **Unique Naming**: Enhancing `get_unique_name` with depth and counter metadata to maintain high subterm uniqueness.
</DESCRIPTION>
