<DESCRIPTION>
To improve `used_vars_proportions_average` (currently 0.48), we modify the "Standard application" branch. Previously, while the `arg_ty` was often selected from the context, the argument term (`a_tm`) was generated via a general call to `gen_term`. This change forces the argument of a standard application to be a variable from the context with a 90% probability if a matching type is available, mirroring the successful strategy used in the redex injection branch. This directly increases variable reuse in non-redex applications.
</DESCRIPTION>
