<DESCRIPTION>
To break the ceiling on `used_vars_proportions_average`, I am implementing a "Variable-Hungry" strategy. 
1.  **Forced Variable Selection**: In the base case (`budget <= 1`), the probability of picking a variable over a literal is increased to 90% if a valid variable exists.
2.  **Context-Aware Argument Types**: When generating an `App` node, the argument type is chosen from the types already present in the context 60% of the time. This ensures that the generated argument branch has a much higher probability of being able to pick a variable from the context, rather than being forced to generate a new literal or a nested term.
3.  **Power-Law Recency Bias**: Replaced simple range-based recency bias with a power-law distribution to more aggressively favor local variables.
</DESCRIPTION>
