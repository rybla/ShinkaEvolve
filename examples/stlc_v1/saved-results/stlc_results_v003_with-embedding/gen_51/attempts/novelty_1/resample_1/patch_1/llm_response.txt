<DESCRIPTION>
Optimizes STLC term generation by intensifying redex frequency and variable reuse. 
1.  **Cubic Recency Bias**: Implements a stricter weighted selection for variables `(index + 1)**3` to favor local scopes.
2.  **Strategic Redex Probability**: Links redex creation directly to the remaining budget to maximize `applied_lambdas`.
3.  **Forced Argument Re-use**: Ensures that 90% of redex applications and 80% of standard applications attempt to use an existing variable from the context, driving `used_vars` significantly higher.
4.  **Asymmetric Budgeting**: Uses a 70/30 split in applications to encourage deep functional nesting.
5.  **Unique Naming**: Implements a more robust 4-part identifier for binders to ensure `unique_subterms` remains near 1.0.
