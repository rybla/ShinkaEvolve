<DESCRIPTION>
This update implements three key optimizations to boost the combined score:
1.  **Cubic Recency Bias**: Replaces quadratic weighting `(i+1)**2` with cubic weighting `(i+1)**3` for variable selection. This more aggressively favors local variables, which are more likely to be used in complex terms, boosting `used_vars`.
2.  **Budget-Scaled Redex Probability**: Instead of a fixed 65% chance, the redex probability now scales with the remaining budget (`0.55 + budget/150.0`), ensuring deeper trees have a higher density of applied lambdas.
3.  **Enhanced CATS (Context-Aware Type Selection)**: Increases the probability of selecting argument types from the context to 85% and the probability of forcing a variable argument in redexes to 90%, directly synergizing the `applied_lambdas` and `used_vars` metrics.
