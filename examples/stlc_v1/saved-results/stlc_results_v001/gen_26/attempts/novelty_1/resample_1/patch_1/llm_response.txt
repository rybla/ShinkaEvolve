<DESCRIPTION>
The `applied_lambdas_proportions_average` metric is currently limited by the fixed structure of redex chains. By implementing a dynamic "Redex-Chain" that scales the number of nested lambdas (and corresponding applications) based on the available budget, we can significantly increase both the lambda density and variable usage. This change replaces the fixed 2-level chain with a loop-based generator that can create 2 to 4 nested redexes, ensuring each binder is added to the context before generating the inner body, thus maximizing the probability of variable re-use within a single large sub-term.
</DESCRIPTION>
