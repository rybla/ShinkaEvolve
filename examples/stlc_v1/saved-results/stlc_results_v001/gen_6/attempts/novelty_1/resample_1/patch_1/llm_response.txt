The current generator is good but can be improved by:
1.  **Increasing Type Complexity**: Setting a fixed higher range for `gen_type` to consistently hit the size 10 limit, which increases `ty_sizes_average`.
2.  **Explicit Variable Capture**: Improving the probability of selecting a variable from the context, especially prioritizing deeper variables to ensure `used_vars_proportions_average` increases.
3.  **Application Density**: Encouraging the creation of `App` nodes where the operator is a freshly generated `Lam` (Beta-redexes), which directly improves `applied_lambdas_proportions_average`.
4.  **Budget Control**: Refining recursion depth and budget distribution to maximize `tm_sizes_average` toward the 100-node limit without exceeding it.
