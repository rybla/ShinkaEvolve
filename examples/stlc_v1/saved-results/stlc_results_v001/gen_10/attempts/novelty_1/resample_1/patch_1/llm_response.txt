To maximize the combined score, we need to increase both the complexity of the terms (`tm_sizes_average`) and the variety of constructs used (`used_vars` and `applied_lambdas`). 

1.  **Iterative Lambda Enclosure**: When the target is a base type, we can wrapping it in an "identity-like" application: `((\x. x) actual_term)`. This directly boosts `applied_lambdas` and `tm_sizes_average`.
2.  **Context Deepening**: Increase the probability of generating `Lam` even for base type targets by generating a `Fun` and immediately applying it.
3.  **Strict Budget Scaling**: Instead of simple division, use a more aggressive budget for the function side of an `App` when we want to find or create complex functions, but ensure the argument also has enough budget to be a non-trivial term.
