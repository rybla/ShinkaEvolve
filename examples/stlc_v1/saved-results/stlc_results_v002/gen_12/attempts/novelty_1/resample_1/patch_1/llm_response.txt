<DESCRIPTION>
This update introduces "Redex-Chain" generation to push the `applied_lambdas_proportions_average` higher. When the budget is high, the generator now has a chance to produce a nested application of a lambda to an argument, creating multiple beta-redexes in a single branch. Additionally, I've refined the variable selection logic to prioritize variables that are functions, which helps in creating more complex `App` nodes and increases the `used_vars_proportions_average`. The budget allocation for general applications is also adjusted to ensure the function part (the operator) receives enough budget to potentially become a complex lambda or another application.
</DESCRIPTION>
