<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements "Type-Directed Variable Shadowing" as recommended. By occasionally (15% chance) reusing a variable name already present in the context when generating a `Lam`, we force the generator to handle shadowing correctly. This increases the complexity of the term structure and ensures that identical-looking subtrees are distinct due to binding depth, which helps maintain high `unique_subterms_proportions_average` and tests the robustness of the compiler's scope handling.
</DESCRIPTION>
