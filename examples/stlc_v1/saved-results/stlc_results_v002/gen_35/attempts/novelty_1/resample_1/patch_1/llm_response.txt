To maximize the `combined_score`, we need to increase `tm_sizes_average` and `applied_lambdas_proportions_average` while maintaining high `used_vars_proportions`. 

The current implementation has a relatively low average term size (13.12). By increasing the initial budget and implementing a "Redex-Chain" (nested applications of lambdas), we can significantly boost the density of applied lambdas. Additionally, by forcing the argument of these redexes to be variables from the context, we ensure the `used_vars` metric remains strong.

3.  **Context-Aware Arguments**: In the redex chain, arguments are prioritized to be `Var` nodes by picking `arg_ty` from the context, which improves `used_vars_proportions`.
4.  **Greedy Budget Allocation**: In `App` nodes, the operator side now receives 85% of the budget (up from 75%) when the budget is high, forcing the operand to be a simple variable or literal, thus saving budget for deeper lambda nesting in the operator.
