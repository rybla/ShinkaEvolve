<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements "Deep Currying" for context lookups and improves variable usage.
1. **Deep Currying**: Instead of only looking for `A -> B`, the generator now searches the context for variables that can produce the `target_ty` after multiple applications (e.g., `A -> B -> C` to get a `C`). This creates nested `App(App(Var, arg1), arg2)` structures which significantly boosts `tm_sizes_average` and `used_vars_proportions_average`.
2. **Variable Selection for Reds**: When generating redexes or general applications, we now prefer types that are more abundant in the context, ensuring that arguments are more likely to be satisfied by `Var` nodes rather than new subtrees.
3. **Budget Rebalancing**: Slightly adjusted the budget distribution in redex chains to ensure the generated arguments have enough depth to be interesting without hitting the recursion limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
