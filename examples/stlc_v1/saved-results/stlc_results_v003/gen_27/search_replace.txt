<DESCRIPTION>
This update introduces two key optimizations to maximize term size and variable reuse.

1. **Spine Depth Offset**: Instead of splitting the budget purely as a percentage, we introduce a fixed offset to the argument side (e.g., `budget - split_f - 2`). This, combined with a slightly higher starting budget (to hit the 100-node limit precisely), ensures the function spine remains extremely deep while the argument branches stay shallow but non-trivial.
2. **Frequency-Weighted Type Selection**: Instead of a uniform choice from the context, we weight the selection of `arg_ty` toward types that appear most frequently in the environment. This significantly increases the mathematical probability that the argument branch can be satisfied using a terminal variable rather than a literal, pushing the `used_vars_proportions_average` higher.
