<DESCRIPTION>
Optimizes the generator by implementing Dynamic Budget-Based Lambda Forcing. This ensures that the generator aggressively builds a deep, variable-rich environment (expanding the context) when the budget is high, while tapering off to allow more diversity as the budget depletes. Additionally, I've incorporated entropy-based literal generation to ensure `unique_subterms_proportions_average` remains near-perfect even with larger terms, and increased the initial budget to push `tm_sizes_average` closer to the limit of 100.
