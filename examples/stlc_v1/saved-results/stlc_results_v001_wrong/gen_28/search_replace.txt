<DESCRIPTION>
The current `Dynamic Redex-Chain` implementation has a structural flaw: it builds the nested lambdas and then applies them in a way that doesn't correctly represent a standard curried application chain (it creates `App(App(Lam, arg1), arg2)` which is good, but the budget distribution and the way it picks `arg_types` can be improved).

I will:
1. Refine the `Dynamic Redex-Chain` to ensure it correctly builds a left-associated application of a nested lambda, which is the most effective way to boost `applied_lambdas_proportions_average`.
2. Increase the `used_vars_proportions_average` by ensuring that arguments to these redexes are pulled from the context with higher probability.
3. Adjust the initial budget and type generation to target slightly larger terms (aiming for the 20-30 range) to improve the `tm_sizes_average` without hitting the 100-node limit.
</DESCRIPTION>
